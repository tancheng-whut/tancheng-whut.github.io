I am a Lecturer at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Wuhan University of Technology. I received both the B.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees in Information Security from Wuhan University. Before taking up my current position, I worked as a Research Assistant at City University of Hong Kong.

My research interests include data security, blockchain security, and AI for software security and coding.
I teach undergraduate courses *Principles and Technologies of Big Data* and *Blockchain Technology*. I also advise undergraduate competition teams. Students interested in information security are welcome to contact me for discussions.

**Research Interests**
- Blockchain Security
- Data Security
- AI for software security and coding

### Selected Publications
